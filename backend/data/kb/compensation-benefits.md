Summary of the text shared by public job postings published on Indeed in November 2026.
Check the specific posting or the official careers website for current details.

## Pay

- Every posting publishes a salary range based on a 35-hour work week. Examples:
  50,900–62,200 (auto claims adjuster, Montréal), 118,700–145,100 (full-stack developer
  Python/React, Toronto), 138,500–169,300 (AI Developer Specialist, Toronto),
  149,600–182,800 (Manager, AI Software Development, Toronto).
- Final salary depends on experience, skills, anticipated contribution and internal equity.
  Candidates outside the range are encouraged to apply.
- Annual bonus target based on base salary (for example 5%, 12% or 15% depending on the role),
  with a potential payout of up to double the target based on personal and company performance.

## Benefits

- Flexible work arrangements and a hybrid work model.
- Option to purchase up to 5 extra days off per year.
- Physical and mental wellbeing benefits, including telemedicine and a wellness account.
- Employee Share Purchase Plan (ESPP): Intact matches 50% of the employee's net shares.
  Share plan and other savings of up to 12% of salary or more.
- Defined benefit pension plan offering guaranteed income for life.

## General conditions

- For roles located in Quebec, bilingualism (French and English) is generally required.
- No Canadian work experience required, but candidates must be eligible to work in Canada.
  No immigration sponsorship is provided.
- Current employees apply through the internal career site.
- Workplace adjustments (accommodations) are available during recruitment on request.
